Deployment Options & Cost Analysis for Leadership

When presenting to your forex broker CEO, the crucial fact is: 1:1 facial verification does NOT require an expensive GPU.

Unlike Large Language Models (LLMs) that require 24GB–80GB of VRAM, facial embedding models like FaceNet (90MB) or SFace (37MB) are compact convolutional networks. A modern 4-core cloud VPS running optimized ONNX Runtime or LiteRT processes facial pairs in under 120 milliseconds per check.

Biometric Data Privacy Policy

Your privacy and regulatory compliance are our highest priority. This service is architected for strict zero-retention biometric compliance under GDPR, CCPA, and BIPA standards.

  • Zero Persistent Storage: Submitted facial images are parsed solely in volatile memory (RAM) for the duration of vector extraction. Images are never written to disk or database.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: All models run entirely on your local machine or private VPS. No data leaves your secure perimeter.
  • Vector Ephemerality: The 512-dimensional vector is used strictly to calculate cosine similarity and is wiped immediately after returning the match payload.
